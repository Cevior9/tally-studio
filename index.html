ClipOps

ClipOps is the in-house publishing tool behind Tally Studio's YouTube channels, starting with Tally Talks.

What it does

  • Finds podcast and interview moments we have permission to use: clipping programmes run by the creators, written licences and Creative Commons Attribution works.
  • Cuts them into short vertical videos with captions, and credits the original creator where the licence requires it.
  • Checks each clip against our rights and content rules, then uploads it to our own YouTube channels through the official YouTube Data API.

Who can use it

ClipOps is not a public service. Only Tally Studio's owner signs in, and it connects only to channels Tally Studio controls.
