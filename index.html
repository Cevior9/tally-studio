ClipOps

ClipOps is the in-house publishing tool behind Tally Studio's YouTube channels, starting with Tally Talks.

What it does

  • Finds moments in source footage supplied by the owner, with stored rights evidence such as a written licence, Creative Commons Attribution terms or public-domain evidence.
  • Cuts them into short vertical videos with captions and the required credits. Campaign material needs its deliverable rules reviewed before processing; campaign discovery and submission are not automated in this version.
  • Checks each clip against our rights and content rules. Its official YouTube Data API connection supports uploading approved videos to our own channels when publication is separately enabled and its safety checks pass.

Who can use it

ClipOps is not a public service. Only Tally Studio's owner signs in, and it connects only to channels Tally Studio controls.

Connecting a channel does not enable uploads. The current installation keeps normal publishing off.
